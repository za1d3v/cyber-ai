What this shows a client: simple, explainable detections catch credential attacks that slip past a firewall. The real work is tuning: at sensitivity 5 analysts face several false alarms for every true one, while at 1 they'd still catch every attack here. False positives are real business travel and ordinary large uploads. Context such as a travel calendar, VPN allow-list or file-sharing logs removes most of them.
