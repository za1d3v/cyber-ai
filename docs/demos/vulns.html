What this shows a client: CVSS measures how bad a flaw could be, not how likely it is to be used against you. Adding exploit likelihood, known-exploitation data and business context lets a small team remove most of the real risk with a fraction of the work. Risk here is measured with the same likelihood × impact model, so read this as "how differently the two orders spend your effort". Turn the model inputs off to see which ones matter most.
